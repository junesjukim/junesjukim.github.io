ChefBox is a subscription service that rents out modular, unmanned stores to food franchises. Our team built the business case for the 3rd SNU × Hansot Food Service Startup Competition. I was CFO: I built the financial model, the unit economics and the market sizing.

### The problem

Opening a franchise branch is expensive and hard to undo. It takes a large upfront investment and depends on a location decision made once; if the neighborhood declines, most of that money is lost. Labor makes it worse: 44.6% of restaurant owners report difficulty hiring, which pushes wages up. And when service depends on staff, one poorly run branch can hurt the whole brand.

### The idea

Instead of buying or building a store, a franchisee subscribes to a fully equipped modular unit. Units go where demand is and can be moved if a location stops performing, so the location risk is much smaller. The store sells private-brand (PB) and home-meal-replacement (HMR) products from partner franchises such as Hansot, so quality stays consistent without cooks on site.

We planned the rollout in two phases. Phase 1 deploys standard vending-style unmanned stores, where the unmanned store market (70% CAGR) meets convenience food (15% CAGR). Phase 2 adds ChefBox Mini, a robotic vending unit that cooks frozen ingredients on site, so it can serve hot meals 24/7. That cooking capability is what would set ChefBox apart from ordinary vending machines.

### The numbers

My job was to show whether a single unit makes money and how large the business could get. Revenue comes from three sources: a recurring subscription fee, a 20% commission on sales, and OEM partnership fees. I built a P&L forecast from operating-cost assumptions and sized the market from the convenience-store lunchbox segment.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue streams | Subscription fee + 20% sales commission + OEM fees |
| Payback period (per unit) | 9 months |
| Operating margin (per unit) | ~52% (≈ ₩7.8M / month) |
| SOM | ₩250B (10% of the convenience-store lunchbox market) |
| Market growth | Unmanned stores 70% CAGR; convenience food 15% CAGR |
| Hiring difficulty | 44.6% of restaurant owners |

### Outcome

The team won a prize at the 3rd SNU × Hansot Food Service Startup Competition and received ₩5,000,000 in funding for prototype development. Industry experts recognized the pitch for how it combined PropTech (modular rental space) with FoodTech (robotic cooking).
